P Pick-A-Book is a flat-pack magazine rack made entirely from corrugated cardboard. The brief was to design a rack that ships flat, assembles without glue or tools, and holds at least ten magazines.
P Its wavy outline is made of five parts: two toothed rings, two plain rings and one slotted wall that wraps around them. Magazines slide into the slots and peek out between the curves, so you can see every cover at a glance and pick the one you want.
H Inspiration
P The rack's wavy outline is inspired by Alvar Aalto's House of Culture in Helsinki, whose curved brick wall bends around the concert hall inside. The slots take after arrow slits, the narrow openings in castle walls that archers shot through: narrow outside, but enough to see what lies behind.

H Looking back
P The final rack was built from materials reused from all of our earlier prototypes, which is why the cardboard does not look its best. If we returned to it, we would source better material and find a cleaner way to cut it than a penknife and the laser cutter.
