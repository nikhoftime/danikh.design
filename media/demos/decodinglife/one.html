what you just experienced

QR codes work because scanners can tell the difference between light and dark. With high contrast, your camera easily separates the pattern from the background. If the colours are too similar, the code disappears into the background.

just like us

Just like our eyes need clear light and shadow to see shapes, machines also need clarity to “see.” The simplest black-and-white design turns out to be the most reliable form of communication.
