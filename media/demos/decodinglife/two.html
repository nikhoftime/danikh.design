what you just experienced

QR codes can self-correct errors — meaning even if up to 30% of the code is damaged or covered, it can still be read.

Every QR code stores extra redundant data using Reed-Solomon error correction. Depending on the level (L, M, Q, H), it can recover 7%, 15%, 25%, or 30% of lost data. This makes it reliable for use in harsh or messy environments.
